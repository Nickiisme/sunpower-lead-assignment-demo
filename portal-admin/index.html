Metric definitions

Scope: Portal-visible leads currently belonging to each installer company, after all query filters. This is a current snapshot, not historical attribution across companies.

First follow-up: the earliest successfully saved follow-up record made by an authorized user in this installer company after the lead entered the company (including the first follow-up that claims a shared lead). Viewing, accepting, assigning, reassigning, or changing status alone does not count. A saved follow-up records an action; it does not prove the customer was reached.

Lead stage completion and conversion rates: all four rates use the same denominator: Total leads belonging to the company in the current query scope. Each lead counts once per metric; the table shows percentage and numerator/denominator. These are cumulative stage completion proportions, not conversion from the immediately preceding stage, and should not be added together.

Claim completion rate: distinct leads with a recorded completion of Claimed after entering the company ÷ Total leads. This includes a shared lead claimed by a regular user's first follow-up. Assignment alone is not claim completion.

Qualification completion rate: distinct leads with a recorded completion of Qualified after entering the company ÷ Total leads. Leads that later close still count if the completion event exists. Current status alone never implies completion of earlier stages; missing milestone records are not counted. Demo milestone events are synthetic examples.

Invalid lead rate: leads currently in Rejected or Disqualified ÷ Total leads, counted once. This provisional grouping treats declined and unsuitable leads as invalid; their separate status counts remain visible.

Final conversion rate: leads currently in Converted ÷ Total leads. Converted requires user confirmation that the lead became a customer. Qualified, Claim completion and closure alone are not final conversion.

Awaiting follow-up: active leads (Pending Claim, Claimed, Qualified) without a valid follow-up record.

Closure: a lead explicitly enters Rejected, Disqualified, or Converted and its closure timestamp is recorded. Rejected means the lead was declined, Disqualified means it was assessed as unsuitable, and Converted means a user manually confirmed conversion to a customer. Pending Claim, Claimed, and Qualified are not closure. A follow-up or reassignment alone does not close a lead.

Avg. first follow-up: average elapsed time from entering the company to first follow-up, over leads with valid timestamps. Unfollowed leads are excluded; read together with the awaiting follow-up count.

Avg. closure time: average elapsed time from entering the company to recorded closure, over leads currently in a closure status with valid timestamps. It combines the three closure outcomes; a shorter time alone does not imply better performance.

Both averages are calculated using full timestamp precision, then rounded to the nearest whole hour (0.5 rounds up). n is the number of valid leads in the average. Durations are elapsed hours, not working hours. Missing timestamps are excluded; no sample displays —, not zero. A rounded 0 h may represent less than 30 minutes.

Company country and company sales representative come from the company profile, independently of a lead’s country or sales representative. These are proposed Demo definitions for business confirmation before production.
